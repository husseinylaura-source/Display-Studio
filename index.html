5 Check and print

  1. Read every piece under Pieces to print. Use Edit text to change any wording.
  2. Drag pieces on the board until you like the layout.
  3. Download the PDF and print it at 100% / actual size on A4 white card.
  4. Cut out each piece. Title letters have a grey cutting line.

The PDF has a layout guide, then one page per title letter, then one page per piece.

How Bulletin Board Studio works

  1. Topic. Type a title and the idea behind your board. Press Write my board content for ready-made facts, questions and prompts.
  2. Colours. Pick the backing paper on your wall and a colour for the pieces.
  3. Layout. Choose one of three layouts. Drag pieces on the preview to move them.
  4. Pictures & extras. Search free pictures, paste from Google, upload your own, add icons and IB cards.
  5. Check & print. Edit any wording, then download one PDF with every piece on its own page.

Your board saves itself in this browser. Use Save board file to keep a copy or send it to a colleague, who can open it with Open board.
